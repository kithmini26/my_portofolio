Know About Me!

Greetings! I'm Vimarshana Kithmini, a BSc (Hons) Software Engineering undergraduate at Sri Lanka Technological Campus (SLTC), focused on Quality Assurance.

My expertise spans manual testing, test case design, bug reporting, and automation with Selenium, Postman, and Qase.io, alongside full-stack development in Python, Java, JavaScript, and SQL.
